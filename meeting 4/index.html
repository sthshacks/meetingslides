How to Win 08 / 09
01 Pick a problem — Recommend picking an idea that you personally have experienced or your community. Judges like it when you can relate the problem to yourself.
02 Create a timeline for your idea — Creating a timeline helps you and your team manage your time effectively without risking running out.
03 Create one thing at a time. — Focus on important or major features first. Then, you can move on to the less critical ones.
04 Debugging — Debug your program to make sure it doesn't have any bugs that would interfere with your presentation
05 Create a pitch for your idea. — Know who will say what and how your general pitch will go. Make sure to memorize to keep the presentation smooth
06 Present to the judge. — Teams will have to present to one or multiple judges. Make sure to maintain eye contact and use body language to your advantage.
