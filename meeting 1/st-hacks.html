What is ST Hacks?

ST Hacks is a computer science club with an emphasis on programming and participation in competitions and hackathons. Most of our practice will be done on LeetCode. Personal and side projects are encouraged, but make sure to stay on top of club projects too, and feel free to ask for help with your projects any time. On top of joining other hackathons, we'll also be hosting some of our own, with prizes.

Quick Definitions 04 / 09

LeetCode

A website with coding practice problems, used to prep for technical interviews and sharpen your problem-solving skills.

Hackathon

An event where teams build a project, usually a website, app, or program, within a set time limit, then present it to judges for prizes.
